HYPOTHESIS TESTING RESULTS
==================================================

Test 1: Independent t-test
H0: mean Total_Sales in East equals mean Total_Sales in West
H1: mean Total_Sales in East differs from mean Total_Sales in West
t-statistic: 2.0623
p-value: 0.0455
Result at alpha = 0.05: reject H0, significant difference
East mean: 132612.58, West mean: 81689.31

Test 2: Independent t-test
H0: mean MonthlyCharges is equal for churned and retained customers
H1: mean MonthlyCharges differs between churned and retained customers
t-statistic: 2.6464
p-value: 0.010079
Result at alpha = 0.05: reject H0, significant difference
Churned mean: 129.77, Retained mean: 111.72

Test 3: Independent t-test
H0: mean Total_Sales is equal for Laptop and Phone products
H1: mean Total_Sales differs between Laptop and Phone products
t-statistic: 0.5650
p-value: 0.5751
Result at alpha = 0.05: fail to reject H0, no significant difference
Laptop mean: 162050.42, Phone mean: 142969.70

Test 4: One-way ANOVA
H0: mean Total_Sales is equal across all four regions
H1: at least one region has a different mean Total_Sales
F-statistic: 2.1644
p-value: 0.0972
Result at alpha = 0.05: fail to reject H0, no significant regional difference

Test 5: Chi-square test of independence
H0: Contract type and Churn are independent
H1: Contract type and Churn are associated
Chi2-statistic: 27.7153
Degrees of freedom: 2
p-value: 0.000001
Result at alpha = 0.05: reject H0, contract type and churn are associated

Normality checks, Shapiro-Wilk
Quantity: W = 0.9304, p = 0.0001, departs from normal
Price: W = 0.9475, p = 0.0006, departs from normal
Total_Sales: W = 0.8989, p = 0.0000, departs from normal
